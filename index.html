Our Philosophy

Dripping in Gold, Smashed to Perfection

At The Cheddarloin, we don't do ordinary burgers. We take 100% prime Angus beef, sear it on high-heat flat tops until deeply caramelized, and cascade it in our signature molten cheddar.

Every bun is custom-baked French brioche, lightly toasted with garlic-herb butter. Paired with our double-fried loaded russets and handcrafted botanical coolers, we deliver a dining experience that turns casual meals into luxury moments.
